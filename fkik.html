Closed-form IK
Wrist-decoupled: the first three joints place the wrist centre, the last three give the orientation. Angles come from (cos, sin) pairs through the raw phase and inverse warp — no arctangent, no angle sums.
CCD (hinge axes)
Each joint rotates about its own fixed axis; u and v are projected onto the plane perpendicular to it, and orientation error is folded into the same (dot, cross) pair.
